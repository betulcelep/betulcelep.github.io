NLP · Sentiment analysis

Yelp Restaurant Review Sentiment Analysis

Uploaded 16 October 2026

Analyses 10,417 Yelp reviews of Bacchanal Buffet in Las Vegas. Reviews are cleaned, language-filtered and lemmatised, then eight classifiers are compared across four text representations; Logistic Regression with n-grams performs best (accuracy 0.83). VADER and TextBlob scores, word clouds and n-gram counts show that complaints centre on long waits and value for money, while praise centres on the food, above all the crab legs.

Regression · Feature engineering

Laptop Price Prediction

Uploaded 13 October 2026

Predicts the price of 1,303 laptop models from their specifications. Raw text columns are turned into usable features: pixel density from screen size and resolution, separate SSD and HDD capacities, CPU groups and clock speed, GPU brand and touchscreen/IPS flags. Five models are compared on log price; XGBoost performs best (R² 0.90), ahead of a GridSearchCV-tuned Random Forest. RAM and SSD capacity emerge as the strongest price drivers.

Clustering · Unsupervised learning

World Military Power 2020

Uploaded 13 October 2026

Groups 138 countries by military capability using K-Means on 28 indicators covering air, land and naval forces, defense spending, manpower and geography. After correcting a data misalignment and treating structural missing values (landlocked and island countries), three profiles emerge: Major Powers, Regional Powers and Limited Capacity. The Global Firepower Power Index, left out of the model, ranks the clusters in the same order, independently validating the result.

Clustering · Customer analytics · SQL

Customer Segmentation with RFM

Uploaded 5 October 2026

Segments 3,054 e-commerce customers for targeted marketing. Raw order data is split into customer, product and order tables in a SQLite database, then summarised as Recency, Frequency and Monetary values and clustered with K-Means (elbow, silhouette and business judgement). Four segments emerge: a loyal group of under 3% of customers brings in a fifth of revenue, while lapsed big spenders hold over half of it, the clearest target for a win-back campaign.

Regression · Marketing analytics

Advertising Spend and Sales

Uploaded 4 October 2026

Multiple linear regression predicting sales from TV, radio and newspaper advertising budgets across 200 markets. The model explains 91% of the variation in sales on held-out test data (RMSE 1.68). Holding other channels constant, radio delivers the largest sales gain per unit of spend, followed by TV, while newspaper spending adds almost nothing.

Clustering · Unsupervised learning

SFO Airline Traffic Profiles

Uploaded 3 October 2026

Groups 65 airlines at San Francisco International Airport by traffic profile, using 13 years of monthly passenger records (2005–2018). Raw records are aggregated to one row per airline and described by passenger volume, domestic, low-fare, transit and long-haul shares. K-Means with elbow and silhouette analysis (silhouette 0.70) identifies six profiles, from long-haul international carriers to low-fare and regional domestic airlines.

Classification · Credit risk

Loan Default Prediction

Uploaded 2 October 2026

End-to-end credit-risk model on 257,000 loan records: deduplication, detection and removal of two target-leakage sources, ratio features (loan-to-income, debt-to-income, credit utilisation) and a comparison of seven classifiers. Logistic Regression is recommended for transparency (ROC AUC 0.70); at a 0.5 threshold it cuts the default rate among approved loans from 27.4% to 17.6%.
