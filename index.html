About

A concise overview of my professional perspective and the type of assignments I aim to support.

My work combines programme design, monitoring and evaluation, stakeholder coordination, quantitative analysis and learning. I have contributed to public grant programmes, regional development planning, project portfolio monitoring and civil-society initiatives. My current portfolio focuses on practical MEL products, including evaluation matrices, theories of change, indicator frameworks, technical papers and Power BI dashboards.

Core expertise

Designing monitoring and evaluation systems that small teams can actually run — and that produce evidence strong enough to defend.

01

MEL System Design

Assessing and rebuilding monitoring systems so they are proportionate to the organisation running them: tiered instruments, indicator sets with a named internal use, and rules for retiring what is no longer used.

02

Theory of Change & Results Frameworks

Making implicit causal logic explicit and agreed, so that outcome structures match what an organisation is actually trying to change rather than what is easiest to count.

03

Evaluation Design & Methods

Evaluation questions, mixed-methods designs and evaluation matrices — including credible treatment of outcomes that resist measurement, stated with their limits rather than over-claimed.

04

Evidence & Data Governance

Data custody and archiving, consent and data-protection protocols, and figures that stay traceable from the field record to the donor report.

05

Grant & Portfolio Monitoring

Technical and financial monitoring, risk review and stakeholder coordination across public grant programmes, with reporting built as a single register that each funder's report filters rather than reconstructs.

06

Gender & Social Inclusion

Intersectional analysis, women's employment and participation, and gender-responsive indicators and disaggregation designed to be used in decisions, not only reported.
